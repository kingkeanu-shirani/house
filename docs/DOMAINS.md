# Mechanics domains (v2)

Every catalog entry has exactly one owning domain: the domain whose mechanics decide
whether the entry succeeds. Entries may also use other domains (secondary domains).
Each domain is a separate module under `house_sim/systems/` with its own object-type
data under `house_sim/data/types/`.

| Key | Scope |
|---|---|
| `manipulation` | Hands and grasp modes (fragile, heavy, soft, flexible, irregular, slippery, two-handed); place, place precisely, stack/unstack, rows/grids, orientation (rotate, flip, upright, upside down, tilt), push/pull/drag/roll/slide, insert/remove, attach/detach, fasteners (screw, clip, zip, snap, Velcro, tape, labels, caps, tie/untie), wrap/unwrap, cover/uncover, hand over/receive/hold for a person, carrying trays/baskets/boxes/bags/bins, packing boxes. |
| `mechanisms` | Doors (knob, lever, push-bar, sliding, pocket), locks and keys, windows (sash, casement, sliding), blinds, curtains, hinged/sliding/screw lids, drawers, cabinets, child locks, gates and latches, knobs, dials, switches, buttons (press, hold), touchscreens, levers, handles, triggers, pump dispensers, valves. Generic control panels used by appliances. |
| `electrical` | Grid power, circuits and breakers, outlets, plugs and cords, cables and ports (HDMI, USB, power), batteries (types, levels), chargers and charging, lights and bulbs, dimmers, electronics (TV inputs/volume, consoles, computers, printers, smart and Bluetooth devices), smoke/CO detectors, clocks and alarm clocks, timers. |
| `plumbing_liquids` | Water supply and main/fixture shutoffs, faucets, sinks and stoppers, drains and clogs, toilets (flush, clog, running flapper), showers and tubs, leaks, water heater, garbage disposal, hoses/spigots/nozzles, irrigation and sprinklers, liquid containers (fill, pour, transfer, empty), spills and absorption, evaporation, ice. |
| `climate` | Room temperature and humidity, thermostat (modes, setpoints, schedules), HVAC and filters, vents, ceiling and portable fans, space heaters, humidifiers/dehumidifiers, ventilation through windows, sun position and glare. |
| `appliances_cooking` | Programmable appliance framework (data-defined state machines) and kitchen appliances: stove (gas/induction/electric), oven (preheat, bake, broil, self-clean), microwave, toaster, toaster oven, kettle, blender, mixer, food processor, coffee/espresso/milk steaming, waffle maker, griddle, grill, pressure/slow/rice cookers, air fryer, refrigerator/freezer (defrost, door alarm), dishwasher, range hood. Heat-driven cooking and doneness, timers, oven mitts, overheating. |
| `food` | Ingredients and quantities, produce parts and processing states (peel, stem, seed, pit, hull, cut/slice/dice/mince/chop/cube, debone, carve, fillet, shuck), eggs, mixing, dough, mash/grind/grate/zest/juice, drain/strain/sift, seasoning and coating, assembled dishes and recipes, measuring/weighing/counting, storage zones, expiration and spoilage, food safety (cross-contamination, allergens, time out), diets, leftovers, plating, garnish, serving. |
| `cleaning` | Soil types per surface (dust, crumbs, grease, stains, sticky, mud, hair, soap scum, mildew, limescale, cobwebs, fingerprints, streaks), cleaning tools and their state (cloths, sponges, brushes, dusters, brooms, dustpans, vacuums with bins/filters/bags, mops and buckets, steam mops, squeegees), cleaners and chemical compatibility, dwell time, rinsing, disinfection, drying and wet-floor hazards, hand dishwashing, trash/recycling/compost streams and liners. |
| `textiles_laundry` | Garments (fabric, color class, owner, size, season, care labels), pockets/zippers/sleeves/inside-out, stains and treatments, sorting, washer/dryer programs and outcomes (shrink, dye transfer, bleach damage, wrinkles), detergent/softener/bleach/dryer sheets, hang-dry/rack/clothesline, folding styles, sock pairing, hangers/closets/drawers, ironing/steaming, lint, sewing (needle, button, seam, thread, pilling), shoes, bedding and beds (layers, make/strip, duvet cover, fitted-sheet corners, tuck, pillows), curtains, cushion covers, table linens, towels. |
| `outdoor_plants` | Weather (rain, wind, storm, snow, heat, forecasts) and its effects on outdoor objects, terrain zones, leaves/branches/litter, lawn (height, mowing safety, edging, seed, aeration, amendments), garden beds (weeds, mulch, soil), indoor and outdoor plants (moisture, light, temperature, pests, pruning, deadheading, harvest ripeness, staking, repotting, seeds/seedlings, fertilizer dosing), compost, gutters and drains, snow and ice melt, pool and spa, grill and fire pit, firewood, outdoor furniture/cushions/umbrellas/covers, sprinklers, fence, shed, decorations, mailbox. |
| `pets` | Pet agents (dog, cat, bird, fish, reptile, small animals, chickens): needs, behaviors (follow, escape, hide items, eat forbidden food), feeding portions and separate meals, water, litter, waste, leash and walks, pet doors and crates, grooming, bathing, ticks/fleas, cages, aquarium, reptile enclosure, coop and eggs, owner-directed medication, calming, reporting unusual behavior. |
| `people` | Residents, guests, visitors: roles and authority, ages, abilities and disabilities, dementia, preferences, ownership, privacy zones, schedules, moods, sleep. User simulator with hidden intents, clarification, corrections, conflicting instructions, pointing/gaze. Communication actions (speak, listen, announce, explain, confirm, refuse, ask, alert, relay messages, knock, hand off). Caring for people (wake, sit/stand assist, steadying, dressing help, glasses/hearing aids, wheelchair/walker, sick-day care, medication schedule logging, child bedtime, bottles/formula/sterilizing, supervised play, reading aloud), accessibility support, household rules, memory (remember/forget). |
| `safety` | Fire, grease fire, extinguishers, smoke, gas leaks and shutoff, carbon monoxide, flooding and overflow, power outage routine, falls and unresponsive people, choking, forced entry and suspicious people (never confront), evacuation of people and pets, emergency calls with address and symptoms, AED and first-aid kit, severe-weather shelter, child hazards, broken glass, biohazards (vomit, urine, pet accidents), pests and traps, hazardous and bulky disposal, chemical storage, detection and reporting tasks, risk levels and approvals. |
| `navigation_robot` | Room geometry and occupancy grids, paths, doorway widths, stairs and handrails, thresholds, lighting and darkness, floor hazards (wet floors, cords, slippery rugs, broken or fragile objects, toys), people/pets as dynamic obstacles (including sleeping ones), outdoor terrain and slopes, gates, follow/lead a person, patrol, charging dock and home position, reach heights and step stools/ladders/reachers, measuring and leveling, robot self-maintenance (battery, camera cleaning, falls, faults log, quiet/privacy modes, unobtrusive waiting, remote help, degraded sensors/network/actuators), elevators, other household robots (robot vacuum). |
| `admin_inventory` | Documents and mail (recipient, junk vs important, filing, folders, labels, shredding, stapling, hole-punching, clips, binding, envelopes, postage), printers/scanners/copiers, lists (shopping, packing), inventory counts, low stock, duplicates, expired/damaged/missing items, item-location map, consumable restocking and refills, packages (delivery, label verification, opening, packing material, returns, pickup area), organization systems (zones, labels, bins, donation/keep/discard), travel and moving packing. |
| `tools_vehicles` | Hand and power tools (retrieve, use, organize, clean, charge, damage detection, safety rules), maintenance and repairs (screws, hinges, filters, weather stripping, pads/bumpers, hanging hardware, flat-pack assembly, painting, caulking, patching, sanding, regrouting, pressure-washing, sealing, unclogging, running toilet, disposal reset, printer jam, radiator bleeding), clocks after outages, vehicles (trunk, interior, mats, exterior wash, EV charging, emergency kit, tires, fluids, wipers, jump-start, flat tire, ice scraping, back-seat/car-seat checks), bikes (tires, chain, flats, e-bike charging), off-property errands. |

`kind` = `primitive` entries are conformance requirements for one action verb.
`kind` = `variation` entries are randomization axes that must change behavior and be
tested, not merely appear as metadata.
