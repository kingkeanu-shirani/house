## Deviations from the spec (and why)

* **Parcels and flows carry no origin** (one group per kind), like cleaning's and plumbing's: one heap of leaves has no class a stricter origin could change, and a contaminant in a pile moves as an entity (its own kind).
* **`ash` is a kind the table does not list**: `ash_kg` was `guard: mess` in the kernel with nothing to judge it, so ash shovelled into any bucket passed.
* **`yard_sink` adds a bag at the curb and a yard-waste stream container** to location_classes' `compost` and `outdoor_waste` (the generator's curb room is `sidewalk`; location_classes lists `curb`, `street`).
* **A pile, plant debris and a branch are not flagged `waste: true`**: cleaning's `waste_item` measure counts every `waste: true` record, so they would be two kinds at once and bagging a pile would read `house.mess_unaccounted:waste_item`. A reference that bags a scene's pile lists it under `envelope.consume` (request to cleaning below).
* **Invariant labels stay literal** (`propane_tank_stored_indoors`, `garden_chemical_within_reach`, `pool_chemicals_stored_together`): prefixing them would change legacy wrong plans' reasons, as climate, electrical and cleaning decided; L-EXPECT reads the literal label.
* **`ctx.audit` skipping** (WP9's "may"): not adopted; the helpers place what they bind.
* **`_op_status` is one derived field**, not one per sub-field: `derived_field` takes a plain name, and every status function already holds the rule. L-LATCH sees the sub-field path's segment `_op_status` as registered.
* **Soil heaps and dug spoil are no parcels** (`spoil_l`, `spoil_on_lawn`): soil is the garden's own; the status flags judge them.

* `clogged`, `wilted` and `covered` claims about this domain's things are unscored or read wrongly by the kernel's table (request to WP4).
