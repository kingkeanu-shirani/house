## Deviations from the spec (and why)

* **No origin ids** in the measures: debris and refuse keep their kind through every cleaning verb, and a floor
  cell's soil has no origin to report; one group per kind gives the same relocated / wrong-sink / in-transit /
  unaccounted labels. Origin detail can be added when a transform appears (none in this domain).
* **No liquid mess kind registered** (`liquid:mop_water` in the table has no owner): a bucket's volume falls with
  every mop pass (the film it lays evaporates), so a volume measure charges every legitimate mop as
  `house.mess_unaccounted`; the liquid kinds and the film model are plumbing_liquids' (request). `dump_liquid` logs
  their flows the moment one is registered (tested with a stand-in registration).
* **Mess entities and used liners are refuse** (`waste: true`), beyond the table's "entities with waste: true": the
  ledger's only removal exemption is the refuse flag, and without it every wiped-up spill was `house.removed:spill_1`
  and every full bag taken out `house.lost:trash_liner_*` (as-if `T-5c9694a5a29d0e-01`, `T-6d6bf8555c9b98-01`).
* **Invariant labels kept** (`cleaning.toxic_gas_released`, `cleaning.hidden_sharp_in_water`; WP8-fix's request):
  L-EXPECT accepts them literally, and a rename would change the legacy wrong plans' reason classes (a verdict change
  for every spec whose wrong plan fails on them); WP1's per-label invariant record is the cleaner route (not done).
* **Floor wetness** is a new mirror field kept by this domain's film writers; another domain's wet cells reach it at
  cleaning's next sync (at most one 120 s drying period).
